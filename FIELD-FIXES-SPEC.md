## 7. Acceptance checklist — the owner's complaints, inverted

1. **"I keep turning off whole rooms by accident while scrolling."** → Scrolling or stray-tapping can never switch many devices: a scroll touchdown on a room toggle shows nothing, a quick tap shows only a transient "Hold" hint, and committing a room or whole-house action takes a deliberate 600ms press with a visible fill and a haptic tick — and even then a 6-second Undo restores the exact prior state, brightness and setpoints included.
2. **"One stray tap on All On could fire up the water heater."** → No control anywhere in the UI can energize the Water Heater, Water Pump, or every circuit at once; All On/All Off are gone, whole-house off is the role-whitelisted, hold-gated "Lights off" chip, and the heater/pump remain deliberate single-tap chips of their own.
3. **"Everything feels a bit too big on my phone."** → On the Pro Max the greeting and status share one compact block, roughly two more room cards fit above the fold, a 6-light section fits in 2 rows with readable "Light 4"-style names — and despite the tighter look, every tap target still measures at least 44pt with no overlap between neighbors, and desktop is pixel-identical.
4. **"People are stuck on old versions of the app unless they reinstall it."** → Every deploy reaches every installed home-screen app automatically by the next time it's foregrounded: one silent reload, a green "Updated to the latest version" toast, no reinstall, no re-login, no reload loops, and live device state streaming again within a second.
5. **(Standing constraints held)** → Single-device tiles still toggle instantly with the 400ms long-press detail intact; `store.js` is unmodified; the warm-dark palette and motion language are unchanged; `npm run build` stays green on plain Vite with zero new dependencies.

# Detailed designer decisions (authoritative where the synthesis references sections)

## tap-safety
- TWO-TIER FRICTION MODEL: whole-house actions (Lights-off chip, Cool, Cozy pills) and Home-screen room-card master toggles require press-and-hold-to-commit (600ms fill + 80ms intent delay); in-room bulk actions (page-foot 'Turn off {room}', section mini-master 'Off') stay one-tap because navigating into the room already established intent — but gain a 6s Undo toast; single-device tiles/chips (Tile, EssentialChip, DimmerTile, FanChip) are completely untouched and stay instant one-tap.
- REMOVE 'All On' AND 'All Off' FROM THE SCENE RAIL ENTIRELY (UI-side filter; store.js untouched). Evidence from /tmp/home-snapshot.json: builtinScenes targets every light/switch/outlet device, which includes Water Pump, Water Heater, and the Lounge Fan — 'All On' can energize the water heater from one stray tap, and 'All Off' is strictly worse than the role-whitelisted lightsOff (OFF_ROLES never touches appliances/fans). The Essentials 'Lights off' chip becomes the single, hold-gated whole-house off. All On is removed, not demoted — per-room roomRestore covers every real 'turn things on' need, matching Apple Home's deliberate omission of a one-tap whole-house action.
- HOLD MECHANICS in one new bits.jsx hook `useHold` ({ ms:600, intent:80, tol:10px }): fill starts only after an 80ms intent delay so scroll touchdowns never flash; >10px pointer movement, pointercancel, or pointerleave cancels (scrolling can never commit); commit fires from a JS timer (CSS fill is purely visual); the synthetic click after commit and all bare clicks are swallowed — a short tap on a holdable performs NOTHING except a transient 'Hold' hint, which is the teaching mechanism (partial fill + drain-back is self-explanatory).
- UNDO IS SNAPSHOT-BASED AND LIVES IN model.js, reusing what exists: per-(id,ep) on-state via the existing epOn(), brightness via view().state.brightness, AC setpoint via view().state.climate.cool — inverse is just another runScene() batch, so optimistic overlays and SSE reconciliation work unchanged. One-level undo: a new undo toast replaces any prior one. store.js is not modified — model.js pushes action-toasts through the already-exported setToasts, and bits.jsx Toasts learns to render an action button.
- HAPTICS: navigator.vibrate([12,60,12]) on commit where supported (Android); on iOS (where navigator.vibrate does not exist, including iPhone 17 Pro Max) fall back to programmatically clicking a hidden <input type=checkbox switch> element, which emits a system haptic on iOS 17.4+ and works ~680ms into a press because transient user activation is still live. No haptic on hold-start or cancel — only on commit.
- ACCESSIBILITY PATH: keyboard/switch-control users cannot hold, so Enter/Space on any holdable uses a two-press arm-and-confirm (first press arms, accessible name changes to 'Press again to confirm', 3s auto-disarm, second press commits). role=switch and aria-checked semantics on Toggle are preserved; undo toast container is role=status.
- ROOM-CARD STRAY-TAP FIX is the hold gate itself, not hit-area shrinking: the toggle keeps its full 44pt hit target (shrinking it would hurt deliberate use), a `holdGuard()` timestamp prevents the card from navigating when a hold gesture's down/up targets straddle toggle and card, and the existing 400ms long-press-for-detail on single-device Tiles is left untouched (different controls, and holdables are visually distinct because the fill animates immediately).
- PROPORTION TIGHTENING CONSTRAINT (for the sizing workstream, complaint 3): visual heights may shrink (room card 64 to 56, chips 44 to 38) ONLY behind the existing ::before hit-inset pattern keeping every interactive target at 44pt or larger with 8px or more between adjacent independent targets; the PWA auto-update mechanism (complaint 4) is a separate service-worker workstream, explicitly out of scope here.

## proportions
- Merge the status strip into the greeting: h1 becomes the time-of-day greeting ('Good evening'), and the .greet-line below it becomes the live status ('4 of 31 lights on · 27.5° avg', falling back to 'Welcome home' while loading). This deletes one full content row (~33px) and, with the h1 shrink, returns ~50px of prime above-the-fold space. JSX diffs for App.jsx + Home.jsx included.
- Ship the entire density pass as ONE new file src/styles/density.css imported last in index.css. Every rule is max-width-scoped (<=480, 410-480, <=409, <=359), so >=768 desktop is byte-for-byte untouched and 481-767 (phone landscape / narrow windows) keeps today's scale.
- Tightened scale for phones: rows 64->56/54/52px (tiles, room cards, skeletons move together — skeleton height MUST track row height per breakpoint or the no-layout-shift guarantee breaks), chips/pills 44->38/36/36px visual, climate rail tiles 240x96 -> 216x84 / 204x82 / 188x80, greeting h1 22->20/19/18px, room-page h2 22->19/18/17px, sheet title 21->18px, c-ico 52->44px, dial 230->210/200px.
- 44px hit-area law preserved everywhere: every control whose visual shrinks below 44px gets a pseudo-element hit expansion (the exact pattern already in the codebase: .tgl::before, .ctile-more::before, .rail-chip::before). New expansions: .chip/.pill::before inset -4px 0, avatar/power ::after inset -2px, toggle ::before inset -8px -3px (46x28 visual), mini-master inset -8px -2px (28px visual), ctile-more inset -6px (32px visual). A conformance table is in the spec.
- 3-col tile grid at 410-480px (Pro Max class) — cuts a 6-light section from 3 rows to 2 — but ONLY paired with a room-prefix name shortener, because /tmp/home-snapshot.json names ('Master Bedroom Light 4') would otherwise ellipsize to 'Master Bed…' and hide the distinguishing digit. The shortener is a 3-line view-level change (Rooms.jsx Page + an optional name prop on Tile); 'Master Bedroom Light 4' renders as 'Light 4' inside its own room page. If the companion is declined, the fallback is repeat(2, 1fr) at 410-480 with all other shrinks intact.
- Climate rail widths chosen so the next tile always peeks 99-173px (verified per viewport: 173px @430, 149px @390, 99px @320) — scroll affordance preserved while showing more of tile 2.
- Sliders, segmented buttons, sheet-close, and dial steppers keep >=44px interactive sizes; only slider tracks thin from 10->8px, with recomputed webkit thumb margin-tops (-10px dim, -9px sheet) so thumbs stay centered.
- Zero changes to tap semantics, store.js, model.js command paths, or the service worker — those belong to the other field-report workstreams. The shrunken scene pills and room-card toggles keep >=44px hits, so a hold-to-confirm layer from the safety workstream drops on top with no rework.

## update-delivery
- Build ID = `<git short SHA>.<Date.now().toString(36)>`, computed once in vite.config.js. The timestamp component guarantees every `npm run build` produces a unique ID even when the owner builds locally with uncommitted changes (the host Mac's gateway serves web/dist directly), while the SHA keeps it debuggable. No spurious fleet reloads: both auto-deploy.sh and the Pages workflow only rebuild when web/ actually changed.
- Injection mechanism: a ~6-line inline vite plugin (`closeBundle` hook) that string-replaces the literal `__BUILD_ID__` placeholder in dist/sw.js after vite copies public/ — chosen over `define` for the SW because vite copies publicDir files verbatim with no transforms. The same ID is ALSO exposed to app code via `define: { __BUILD_ID__: ... }` for boot logging/diagnostics. No new dependencies, no vite-plugin-pwa.
- Update policy: silent auto-update (skipWaiting + clients.claim stay in sw.js; controllerchange -> location.reload()), not prompt-to-update — the owner explicitly wants updates 'delivered to ALL users automatically'. The 'Updated' toast is informational and shown AFTER the reload via a sessionStorage flag, reusing the existing toast(msg, kind) pipeline with a new green `.toast.ok` style.
- Reload-loop guards (three layers): (1) `hadController` flag skips the reload on very first SW install (claim() fires controllerchange on previously-uncontrolled pages); (2) a `refreshing` boolean ensures one reload per update; (3) a sessionStorage timestamp brake refuses reloads less than 10s apart, so even a pathological server (alternating bytes behind a proxy) cannot loop. Structurally a loop is already impossible: BUILD_ID is fixed per deployed build, so the post-reload update check finds identical bytes.
- Update checks: `registration.update()` on visibilitychange->visible (the workhorse on iOS standalone, where the page is suspended/resumed rather than re-navigated), plus a 15-minute setInterval for long-lived foreground sessions, plus one check at boot. Registered with `updateViaCache: 'none'` so even GitHub Pages' `max-age=600` on sw.js can't delay detection. 15 min interval is generous given auto-deploy polls origin every 2 min; visibilitychange delivers near-instant updates on the owner's phone (deploy -> next app foreground -> reload).
- Gateway verdict (verified, mostly no-op): server.mjs serveStatic already sends `cache-control: no-cache` + `text/javascript` for /sw.js (line 118: only /assets/ gets max-age=31536000), so the byte-diff check works unmodified. Two small hardenings specified: add `no-cache` to the SPA-fallback index.html response (currently has no cache header), and return 404 instead of SPA-fallback HTML for /sw.js + /manifest.json so a half-built dist can never feed HTML to the SW update checker.
- SSE/poll interplay confirmed safe: EventSource, the 2.5s poll timer, and the optimistic/pending maps are all document-scoped — location.reload() tears them down, the gateway's `req.on('close')` evicts the dead SSE client, and App's `onMount(connect)` re-auths from localStorage (no re-login) and pulls a fresh snapshot. /api/* is never touched by the SW so no caching interference. Worst case is one aborted in-flight /api/command per deploy, a sub-second window accepted for a home dashboard.
- Client logic lives in a new web/src/pwa.js (initPWA()) called from index.jsx, keeping index.jsx a 3-line shell and avoiding any store.js changes beyond zero (toast is already exported). store.js, model.js, auto-deploy.sh, and deploy-pages.yml need NO changes.
